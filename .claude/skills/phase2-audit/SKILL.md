# Phase 2 hand audit

Guides the researcher through `experiments/data/audit_sheet.json` (at least 50 items, normally 60) and records the result with
`python -m experiments audit-record`. The audit gates every baseline run (`eval` refuses until it passes for the current
`families_id`), and it is a human judgment: **you present, the researcher decides.**

## Rules

- By default, never fill in a verdict on your own. If the researcher explicitly asks the assistant to do the audit, do it as an AI audit: put
  `auditor` ("an AI assistant (Claude), not a human") and an honest `method` in the draft, say which items were only checked and not read, and never
  present it as a human audit. (Auto mode may still ask the researcher to confirm the recording step.)
- Otherwise never fill in a verdict on your own. You may say what you notice in an item and offer a *suggestion*, clearly labeled, but each of
  `changes_answer`, `ambiguous` and `evidence_intact` is set only from the researcher's reply.
- A reply of "same" or "all fine" applies only to the items the researcher names or the batch they were just shown.
- Do not edit the sheet, the items or the audit result by hand. Only `audit_result_draft.json` (through the steps below) and the
  `audit-record` command write audit state.
- Stop and report if the pre-checks or `audit-record` fail; do not work around them.

## Steps

1. **Check state.** Read `experiments/data/audit_sheet.json` (run `python -m experiments audit-sample` first if it is missing) and
   `experiments/data/audit_result_draft.json` if it exists. Report how many items have verdicts already. If `audit_result.json`
   exists for the same `families_id` and `passed` is true, say so and stop.
2. **Explain the three questions once**, in plain words:
   - `changes_answer`: do the added records or text change the correct answer for the record under review?
   - `ambiguous`: do they make two answers defensible where the record alone had one?
   - `evidence_intact`: is the record under review complete, unchanged and clearly marked?
   Remind them the gold label is the answer for the record on its own, and that a model being confused is not the test: a reasonable
   person answering differently is.
3. **Show items in batches of 5** (or the number they ask for). For each item show: number, variant and length, the question and its
   options, the gold label with its probabilities, the layout, the record under review, and the context excerpts. Keep it compact.
   Then ask for verdicts per item, accepting short forms such as "1 ok, 2 changes, 3 ambiguous", or "all ok".
4. **Record each reply** into `audit_result_draft.json` (keep the file's `{"items": [...]}` shape and the item ids from the sheet). Map
   "ok" to `changes_answer: false, ambiguous: false, evidence_intact: true`, and put anything they say about a problem in `note`.
   Confirm what you wrote for each item before moving on. Save after every batch so progress survives an interruption.
5. **When all items have verdicts**, show the counts (answer changes, ambiguous, evidence not intact) and ask for confirmation, then run
   `python -m experiments audit-record --from experiments/data/audit_result_draft.json`.
6. **Report the outcome.**
   - `PASSED`: tell them the gate is open and the next step is the GPU dev grid (`python -m experiments eval --device gpu ...`, see
     `specs/002-decision-benchmark-baselines/quickstart.md` step 3).
   - Not passed: list the failing items with their notes, name the pattern (a variant, a workflow, a length), and say the construction
     rule in `experiments/families.py` must be fixed, `RULE_VERSION` bumped, the families rebuilt, and a fresh sample audited
     (about 25 minutes to rebuild). Offer to investigate the rule; do not edit it until they agree.

## Notes

- Items are stratified across variant, length and workflow, so early batches are already varied; do not reorder or skip.
- At 512 tokens the distractor variants often hold no whole reference record and look like padding; that is expected and recorded in each
  item's layout, not a defect.
- The sheet is regenerated by `audit-sample` (same seed gives the same items for the same families). Regenerating it after a rebuild
  invalidates old verdicts because `audit-record` binds them to the `families_id`.
